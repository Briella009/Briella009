WHY THIS MATTERS

Delayed care is not a small problem.

62.3%of 138 women in one Ibadan fibroid clinic study presented six months or more after symptoms began.
35.8%of 137 participants with treatment-timing information began treatment more than three months after diagnosis.

Single-clinic, selected sample. These are not national prevalence estimates. Read the study.

OUR PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Care navigation, not diagnosis.

GynNavi does not determine what a symptom means, interpret scans independently, choose treatments, or replace a licensed clinician.

  • Original report text stays visible
  • No unsourced medical conclusions
  • Appointments and next steps are user-controlled
  • Research limitations are stated clearly

NIGERIA / PRIMARY STUDY
Late presentation of symptomatic uterine fibroids
138 participants at UCH Ibadan; 62.3% presented after six months or more from symptom onset. Among 137 with treatment timing, 35.8% commenced treatment more than three months after diagnosis.
Limitation: Selected clinic sample of women aged 20-49 with at least secondary education; findings are not nationally representative or evidence that an app reduces delay.
Read open-access study ↗
CLINICAL / PATIENT EDUCATION
ACOG: Uterine Fibroids
Defines common fibroid symptoms and explains assessment and available management approaches. Suitable as a clinician-reviewed educational reference, not a personalised treatment engine.
Review ACOG guidance ↗
